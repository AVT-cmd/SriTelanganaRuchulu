Authentic Generational Culinary Heritage

Welcome to Sri Telangana Ruchulu, your authentic online destination for handcrafted traditional Telangana and Andhra delicacies. Sourced and prepared fresh daily in our Warangal kitchen, our artisanal culinary artisans follow authentic recipes passed down through generations. Red chilies are sun-dried, cold-pressed sesame and groundnut oils are pressed in wooden mills, pure cow ghee is slow-churned, and spices are pounded in traditional stone mortars (Rolu) without artificial colors, synthetic flavors, or chemical preservatives.

Traditional Product Specialties

  • Veg Pickles (Pachadlu): Authentic Gongura Pachadi, Traditional Avakaya Mango Pickle, Spicy Allam Pachadi, Usirikaya, Tomato Pickle, and Chintakaya.
  • Festive Pure Ghee Sweets: Pure Ghee Ariselu, Thati Bellam Palli Laddu, Badhusha Neyi, Madatha Kaja, Kobbari Burelu, and Poornalu.
  • Stone-Ground Spice Podulu: Munagaku Podi, Gunpowder Kandi Podi, Karivepaku Karam, Vellulli Karam, and Royya Pottu Karam.
  • Non-Veg Pickles: Spicy Telangana Chicken Pickle, Boneless Mutton Pickle, Prawns (Royyalu) Pachadi, and Fish Pickle.
  • Hot Snacks (Pindi Vantalu): Traditional Telangana Sakinalu, Karam Sakinalu, Pappu Chekkalu, Murukkulu, Sarva Pindi, and Chegodilu.
