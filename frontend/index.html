Sri Telangana Ruchulu - Authentic Traditional Pickles, Sweets & Snacks

Welcome to Sri Telangana Ruchulu, your authentic online destination for traditional Telangana and Andhra culinary delicacies. Handcrafted fresh in Warangal, Telangana, our kitchen prepares authentic veg pickles (Avakaya, Gongura, Allam), mouth-watering non-veg pickles (Chicken, Mutton, Fish, Prawns), festive pure ghee sweets (Ariselu, Mysore Pak, Sunnundalu, Laddus), crunchy hot snacks (traditional Sakinalu, Murukkulu, Sarva Pindi), and stone-ground spice podulu.

Explore Traditional Product Categories

  • Veg Pickles - Authentic Gongura Pachadi, Mango Avakaya, Allam Pachadi, Usirikaya, Tomato & Chintakaya.
  • Traditional Sweets - Pure Ghee Ariselu, Thati Bellam Palli Laddu, Badhusha, Madatha Kaja, Kobbari Burelu & Poornalu.
  • Authentic Podulu - Munagaku Podi, Kandi Podi, Karivepaku Podi, Vellulli Karam & Royya Pottu Karam.
  • Non-Veg Pickles - Spicy Chicken Pickle, Boneless Mutton Pickle, Prawns (Royyalu) Pachadi & Fish Pickle.
  • Hot Snacks (Pindi Vantalu) - Traditional Telangana Sakinalu, Karam Sakinalu, Pappu Garalu, Murukkulu & Chegodilu.
